Schedule

Meeting schedule for the CofC Cybersecurity Club.

During Fall/Spring semesters the club meets twice a week, Tuesday and Thursday at 5:30 PM in the Simons Center for the Arts, Room 281. Tuesdays are general-discussion meetings covering a cybersecurity topic, sometimes with guest speakers. Thursdays are Cyber Defense Team meetings, going deeper on a topic and preparing for competitions. This page is subject to change; updates are posted on Discord.
