Schedule

Meeting schedule for the CofC Cybersecurity Club.

During Fall/Spring semesters the club meets twice a week, Tuesdays and Thursdays. Tuesdays are general-discussion meetings covering a cybersecurity topic, sometimes with guest speakers. Thursdays are Cyber Defense Team meetings, going deeper on a topic and preparing for competitions. Exact time and location for each meeting are in the table below — they occasionally vary by week, so don’t rely on memory. This page is subject to change; updates are posted on Discord.
